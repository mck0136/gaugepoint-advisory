Technology judgment

Knowing when not to use AI is part of readiness.

Gaugepoint teaches leadership teams how to distinguish a real AI opportunity from a process, management, or systems problem that should be solved another way.

01The process is not stable or understood.

Standardize the work before automating it.

02The decision requires accountable human judgment.

Use technology to improve the evidence, not remove the owner.

03The data or integration cannot support a dependable result.

Fix the operating foundation first.

04The economics do not justify the change.

Choose the simplest intervention that improves performance.
