The useful question

What operating outcome should improve?

AI is useful when it improves a real operating decision or reduces the effort required to move information through a workflow. That can mean reading a tender, forecasting capacity, coordinating an exception, or verifying a physical event in a yard.

The important question is not which model is most advanced. It is whether the process, information, controls, people, and economics support a better outcome.

Human in the loop

Accountability is an operating design, not a slogan.

A useful control model defines what the system may recommend, what it may execute, when approval is required, what evidence a person sees, and how exceptions escalate.

AI agents should reduce waiting, chasing, repetitive follow-up, and administrative work without removing accountability.

From idea to investment case

Start with a baseline. End with a scale decision.

Define

Name the operating problem

Identify the labour, cycle time, service failure, error, lost revenue, or margin leakage that matters.

Design

Specify the workflow and controls

Map the people, information, systems, exceptions, permissions, and approval model required.

Measure

Prove a useful change

Set the baseline, pilot scope, adoption requirement, operating cost, and evidence needed to justify scale.
