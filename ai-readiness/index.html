Common triggers

When an assessment becomes useful.

AI pressure without direction

  • Leadership is receiving competing proposals.
  • Employees are using ungoverned public AI tools.
  • Pilots exist but cannot scale.

Operating constraints

  • Critical workflows remain manual and email-driven.
  • Data exists but is not trusted.
  • The TMS and operating systems do not communicate well.

Investment questions

  • Management cannot establish a defensible ROI.
  • Growth is adding administrative labour at the same rate.
  • Ownership needs a practical roadmap before approving investment.
