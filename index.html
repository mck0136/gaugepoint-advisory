The Gaugepoint AI Readiness System

Seven conditions. One operating system.

Readiness is the alignment required to produce measurable Value, not a technology score.

Operating outcome Measurable
Value

A defined business problem comes first.

Clarify the operating outcome, executive owner, and evidence that will define success before choosing a tool.

Understand the work before automating it.

Map the normal flow, the exceptions, the handoffs, and the decisions that determine performance.

The operation must trust the evidence.

Identify the information required, where it lives, how current it is, and who verifies it.

The business case must survive implementation.

Connect the workflow to a credible baseline, the cost of change, and a measurable operating result.

Technology must connect to the operating environment.

Confirm integration, permissions, system ownership, and the path from recommendation to action.

Adoption is an operating requirement.

Involve the people who run, sell, price, dispatch, finance, and support the work.

Authority and accountability remain explicit.

Define what the system may recommend, what a person must authorize, and what evidence is retained.

Technology judgment

The right technology for the right workflow.

Gaugepoint helps leaders distinguish an AI opportunity from a process, management, or systems problem. The answer may be probabilistic AI, deterministic automation, business-process redesign, or another technology better suited to the work.

Do not use AI when:

  1. 01

    The process is unstable. Standardize the work first.

  2. 02

    The decision requires accountable judgment. Improve the evidence, not remove the owner.

  3. 03

    The data cannot support a dependable result. Fix the operating foundation.

  4. 04

    The economics do not justify the change. Choose the simplest useful intervention.
